GRAPHIUM G05 SEARCH SCALE HARDENING RECEIPT
Date: 2026-08-14
Status: PRE-DESKTOP ENGINEERING EVIDENCE; NOT T480 CERTIFICATION

DEFECT CLASS FOUND BEFORE CANDIDATE
1. Document-wide Unicode boundary mapping could amplify a 10 MiB search command to about
   6.8 seconds and about 130 MiB of temporary allocation when a length-changing casefold
   character was present.
2. Find Next/Previous initially materialized find_all(), allowing frequent queries to
   allocate a SearchMatch object for every occurrence.

REJECTED DESIGN
- whole-document casefold/boundary map;
- navigation by complete occurrence-list materialization.

ADOPTED DESIGN
- query/replacement remain single-line;
- full Unicode casefold correctness retained;
- transformed/source boundary mapping is logical-line bounded under G04 renderability;
- Find Next/Previous locate only the requested occurrence;
- Replace All alone freezes matches and is capped at 50,000;
- independent DeltaHistory payload cap remains fail-closed before mutation.

LOCAL POST-REPAIR MEASUREMENTS (container engineering host, not T480):
- 10 MiB case-sensitive Find Next to near EOF: about 6 ms in initial spot measurement;
- 10 MiB case-insensitive Find Next: about 250 ms in initial spot measurement;
- same 10 MiB fixture with one ß length-changing fold: about 250 ms;
- dedicated g05_search_performance.py subsequently measured median roughly 116-118 ms
  for 10 MiB case-insensitive workloads on this host and under 60 ms for 10 MiB moderate
  Replace All; exact T480 values remain pending.
