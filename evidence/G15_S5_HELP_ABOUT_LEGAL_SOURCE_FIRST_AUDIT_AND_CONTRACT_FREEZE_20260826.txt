4. GPL-3.0-OR-LATER AUTHORITY

- SPDX expression: GPL-3.0-or-later
- Copyright © 2026 leviagravia
- Author: leviagravia@zohomail.eu

GTK3 authority:
- Gtk.License.GPL_3_0 / GTK_LICENSE_GPL_3_0 means GNU GPL version 3.0 OR LATER.
- Therefore About must use Gtk.License.GPL_3_0, not CUSTOM and not GPL_3_0_ONLY.

Repository license contract:
- add exactly one top-level LICENSE authority
- LICENSE begins with the Graphium project grant explicitly stating "either version 3 of the License,
  or (at your option) any later version"
- the unchanged full GNU GPL version 3 license text follows that project notice
- no second COPYING/LICENSE duplicate is introduced
- installer copies LICENSE into the installed private Graphium root so installed redistribution retains it
- no network fetch of license text
- no new dependency/module/service

Project notice semantic content is frozen as:
  Graphium
  Copyright © 2026 leviagravia

  Graphium is free software: you can redistribute it and/or modify it under the terms of the GNU
  General Public License as published by the Free Software Foundation, either version 3 of the License,
  or (at your option) any later version.

  the implied warranty of MERCHANTABILITY or FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE. See the GNU General
  Public License for more details.

  The full GNU General Public License version 3 follows.

The GPLv3 body must be taken byte-for-byte as legal text from the mature local GPLv3 corpus
(FeatherPad/GNOME Text Editor COPYING), not rewritten or summarized.
