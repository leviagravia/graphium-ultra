SOURCE-FIRST / MATURE FINDINGS
- FeatherPad uses Ctrl+Shift+U for Uppercase and Ctrl+Shift+L for Lowercase.
- GTK3 owns Ctrl+Shift+U for Unicode code-point input; therefore FeatherPad's Uppercase binding is rejected for Graphium.
- Mousepad 0.7.0 leaves Uppercase/Lowercase without direct accelerators and uses Ctrl+Alt+U for Opposite Case.
- gedit Change Case plugin leaves Uppercase/Lowercase without default accelerators.
- Kate provides a mature precedent for Ctrl+U = Uppercase.
- Ctrl+Alt+L is rejected because Cinnamon uses it for Lock Screen; Ctrl+Alt+letter is also disfavored for international/AltGr input.
- Chord schemes such as Ctrl+K,Ctrl+U / Ctrl+K,Ctrl+L are rejected as unnecessary complexity for Graphium.
